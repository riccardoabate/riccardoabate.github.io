Mathematics

Mathematics is the language of Nature. We can see the beauty of it everywhere, from the symmetries in a molecule to the edges of a snowflake. I am particularly interested in learning more about operator theory, group theory, PDEs, differential geometry and algebraic geometry.

Physics

If Mathematics is the language of Nature, then Physics must be its poetry. Physics is in everyday life. It is not simply something we can spot, but rather the very definition of our reality - or, at least, the way in which reality presents itself to us. My interests lie in Quantum Mechanics, Quantum Chromo-Dynamics, Mathematical Physics and Particle Physics.
